In the [[The Nine Hells]], Styx is the layer of the most vile. A frozen wasteland with thick layers of ice covering everything in ice. Floating in the sky are countless boulders of ice. Inside, encased completely, are the conscious and aware souls of the damned. They cannot move, breathe, or even see. However, they still feel the icy burn of their tomb and spend eons trapped, unable to do anything but stay trapped in the ice, unable to die, unable to live. The plane is ruled by the [[Archdevils|Archdevil]] [[Brlglonteth]] and the [[Demon Lords|Demon Lord]] [[Tyrolethotep]].
