In the [[The Nine Hells]], Carnal is the layer of vices. Carnal is a vast city of winding, narrow, dank alleyways, filled with lustful addicts and fiends. The only light comes from dumpster fires and lit cigarettes. Sex is everywhere, drugs and violence are abound, and the air is thick with the reek of sweat and smoke. The plane is ruled by the [[Archdevils|Archdevil]] [[Agares]] and the [[Demon Lords|Demon Lord]] [[Graz'zt]].
