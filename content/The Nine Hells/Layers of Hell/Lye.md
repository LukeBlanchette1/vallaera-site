In the [[The Nine Hells]], Lye is the layer of heretics. Lye is rocky, jagged, with mineral-filled mountains filled with slaves providing supplies to the [[Blood War]]. Those that disrespect or tarnish the image of the gods are sent here to be bound in chains and work as a slave. The plane is ruled by the [[Archdevils|Archdevil]] [[Adauin]] and the [[Demon Lords|Demon Lord]] [[Lalzonanac]].
