In the [[The Nine Hells]], Labyrinthine is the layer of greed. Labyrinthine is an infinite expanse of winding stone tunnels devoid of all life and decor. Its residents are starved and thirsty, unable to find food or drink, shelter, anything. They wander around its empty, backrooms-esque, corridors deprived of anything. On the rare chance they run into another creature, the interactions are often violent and brief. The plane is ruled by the [[Archdevils|Archdevil]] [[Nomuma]] and the [[Demon Lords|Demon Lord]] [[Fraz-Urb’luu]].
