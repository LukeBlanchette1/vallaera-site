The Nine Hells are the scar left on the cosmology in the wake of the [[Purgation]]. The primary battleground in the war between the [[Atzani]] and the [[gods]] was held where the Nine Hells currently resides. The utter devastation left the cosmology permanently deformed. The only way to enter or exit the Nine Hells is through Avernus, its first layer. Plane shift and other forms of planar transportation are inoperational unless granted permission by the gods or the ruler of Hell, [[Asmodeus]] himself.  There are nine layers of Hell, thus the naming, the Nine Hells. Each layer feeds into the next, creating a ladder of reality you can ascend or descend. 

The Nine Hells are primarily occupied by two forces, the demons and the devils.  These two groups are locked in a vicious and gruesome war known as the [[Blood War]]. Both groups believe themselves to be the rightful denizens of the Hells. Located on each layer is an [[Archdevils|Archdevil]] and a [[Demon Lords|Demon Lord]] who are diametrically opposed to one another and lead conquests against each other.

Also located on each layer of the planes are the souls of sinners from various other planes. Each layer has its own respective sin, and the souls that go there are bound to that plane forever, unable to be brought back by resurrection magic. Only the Archdevil of that layer or Asmodeus himself can permit a soul to go free from the hellish punishment, and only for a steep price…
