$\quad$A warped, leather book radiating pure evil, the Soulreaper’s Requiem was created by 
a vile necromancer. It traps the souls of people inside of it and allows for their bodies to be resurrected and remotely controlled by the wielder of the book. It was historically used to entrap the souls of powerful warriors and reanimate them under the user’s control. However, it was secured by Meteor Mouskovitz after he defeated its last user, the Dark Lich Malakar. It is currently locked away under Mouskovitz’s protection so no others can claim it…
