$\quad$A small bottle forged by a following of a hundred orcish smiths worshiping Shavteus, 
who perished during its creation. Legends speak of a flaming liquid held inside that could grant nigh immortality if the drinker can survive the ordeal of consuming it. It is said that every 100 years, the magical substance inside Grishimanen is replenished. Long lost by the paranoid warmonger Kubrelka, Grishimanen has no records of appearance in centuries...
