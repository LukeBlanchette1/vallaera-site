$\quad$A crude, ivory flute fashioned with a dragon head on the end of it. It was created by the ancient hero Hypellos from a shard of Nairutt’s horn. When played, it grants its wielder control over dragons, similar to a snake charmer’s melodies. The flute was used to dominate lands and conquer countless empires as the control of dragons made its wielder nearly invincible. Ascarberis was despised by the dragons and unified both chromatic and metallic dragons alike against its wielder. Ascarberis is currently in the possession of one of the great metallic dragons of Vallaera, although the exact dragon is unknown. Many theorize the metallic dragons shift Ascarberis’s location frequently to prevent anyone from pinpointing its exact location…
