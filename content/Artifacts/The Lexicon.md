$\quad$An eldritch board piece tied to the Atzani. It allows its wielder to learn the 
Ancient Tongue, the language of the Atzani, that is, if their mind doesn’t break from its presence. Learning the Ancient Tongue allows the wielder to channel eldritch, horrific powers unfathomable to the mind. The wielder gains immense power, but often goes mad alongside it. The Lexicon was lost when the Mad Mage Kygrac was slain and the Lexicon was thrown deep into the depths of the ocean, although its existence stirs up cults in coastal sewers who investigate its whereabouts…
