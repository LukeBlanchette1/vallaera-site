The Day of Deliverance commemorates [[Drokai]]’s defeat at the hand of The [[Founders]]. Their heroic battle and sacrifice is celebrated across [[Vallaera]] with 2 days of partying and specific art depicting the grand battle. Songs, plays, poems, paintings, and sculptures are all created to celebrate the heroism of the First All-Stars. This is also the day that [[The League of All-Stars]] renews their contracts with the nations of the world, always re-signing them.
