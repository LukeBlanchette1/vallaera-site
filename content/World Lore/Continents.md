The world of [[Vallaera]] has 9 continents that hold civilization. These 9 continents are Castkia the frigid Continent, Tavra the metropolitan continent, Kalistis the arid continent, Shijuthu the tropical continent, Motu the maritime continent, Paladis the arcane continent, Ayuma the artisan continent, Corkus the automation continent, and Anatha the warrior continent.
