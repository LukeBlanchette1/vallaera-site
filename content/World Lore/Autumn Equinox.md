*On the day of the Autumn Equinox, you may notice a mysterious chill run down your spine or a loud bump in the night. On the day of the Autumn Equinox, shadows grow longer and hold passage to The Shadowfell. On this day the denizens of the Shadowfell can traverse the border between the planes and come to the Material Plane. Cultures across Vallaera have developed festivals or traditions for the Autumn Equinox in order to either satisfy or scare off their shadowy visitors...*
