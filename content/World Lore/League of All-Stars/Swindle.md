$\quad$Swindle is a High Elf Wizard, the son of Fleece. He was raised by his mortal high elven mother who passed down her proclivity for arcana to her son. His mother would tell him tales of his father and his exploits which Swindle sought to emulate as he grew older. Swindle traveled the world, studying the unique magic and magical items of each place he visited. Swindle eventually mastered chronurgy magic to be like his father. Swindle used this power to steal and grab powerful magic items that he thought could help him be powerful enough to save his father from his fate and be with him again. Swindle has a reputation across Vallaera as one of the most powerful wizards in the world. This great talent eventually gained him the role of Exemplar. As an Exemplar he has access to great resources but is quite frugal with his vast wealth. Swindle is currently the Exemplar stationed in Shijuthu.
