The League of All-Stars is the primary global peace keeping operation. The League currently has around 30 All-Stars and claims “The World’s Strongest Party.” The League of All-Stars handles threats that are too powerful for nations to even deal with. The All-Stars battle with dragons, krakens, and any manner of creature regularly. They also deal with humanitarian crises such as unjust wars or natural disasters.

The League of All-Stars is led by Orpheus, their commander. Orpheus directly leads the [[Exemplars]], 7 of the strongest heroes in the world. Orpheus and the Exemplars have achieved something of a celebrity status across the world of Vallaera for the level of their heroics and the title of All-Star. Under the guidance of The All-Star League, Vallaera has remained relatively stable for over 600 years following The [[Day of Deliverance]]. An invitation to the League of All-Stars is the highest honor a hero can be granted.

The League of All-Stars was founded by [[Enzio Kerrigan]] after the [[Tenday of Ash]]. Enzio saw a need to maintain a unified heroic force to defend the Material Plane from evil.
