$\quad$Not much is known of Serana’s past. The people of Vallaera know Serana was born in the Markessian Triumvirate of Tavra and orphaned as a child. She grew up in the streets of the Markessian capital. She is trained in every weapon and is known to be a formidable fighter. Her unique mastery over the martial arts earned her the position of Exemplar. She is known not to speak much and enjoy sweets. Serana is currently the Exemplar stationed in Tavra.
