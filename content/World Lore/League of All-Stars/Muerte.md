$\quad$Muerte hails from the The Dunes of Castiel of Ayuma. He was born to a Goliath mother and Fire Genasi father which led to his somewhat peculiar nature. To pair with his unique ancestry, Muerte was also born in a high density zone of magic in the Dunes. Being born during a mirage transition in The Dunes meant that Muerte became unstuck in the fabric of space. Muerte’s parents raised him with the idea that he would be a great hero with his special gifts. Muerte was trained by his Goliath mother and honed his abilities from a young age. He was sent to The Moonbringer Academy of Martial Arts where he graduated with High Honors in only 2 years due to his immense talent. During his time at MAMA, he became obsessed with his own strength and maximizing his potential. Muerte became renowned as one of the saviors in The Dunes as he would save lost adventurers when he came across them. He was even powerful enough to contend with some of the local magical wildlife. These tales eventually got back to Orpheus who knew he needed Muerte in the League of All-Stars. Eventually, Muerte was able to earn his way into his current role as an Exemplar. Muerte is currently the Exemplar stationed in Ayuma.
