**$\quad$Vinsted Toshiro was born in Numi no Kuni of Motu. Vinsted was born into a druidic conclave. His conclave was focused on the powers of the moon and its power over nature. Vinsted was a prodigy of these teachings and far outpaced his peers as he grew up, eventually becoming one of the strongest druids of the conclave. Eventually, a band of pirates invaded his home and left nothing but destruction in their wake. Vinsted fought bravely but could not find any other survivors. Distraught and alone, Vinsted began wandering Motu, trying to find possible refugees from the battle. Vinsted traveled across the diverse environment of Motu. While he traveled, Vinsted would often help the towns he came across. Whether this was growing goodberries, healing the sick, or wrangling up food, he made a difference as he trekked the continent and became something of a folk legend. One day, while adventuring through the Western Coast of Ilhanus, Vinsted came across a small coastal village being attacked by a Sea Serpent. Vinsted was able to single handedly defeat the beast but sustained enough damage that he almost died in the attempt. Lucky for him however, Orpheus had arrived to deal with the Sea Serpent for the All-Star League. Orpheus was impressed by Vinsted’s bravery and saved him from death. When Vinsted awoke, Orpheus invited him to the League of All-Stars which Vinsted accepted. Vinsted eventually earned his way to his current status as an Exemplar. Vinsted is currently the Exemplar stationed in Motu.
