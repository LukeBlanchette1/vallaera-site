$\quad$Ramiel was born in the Dozal Federation of Anatha. Ramiel was born in the borderlands where his family had to fight for everything they had. Ramiel grew up with his younger sister, Delilah. Through growing up in the harsh environment of the Dozal Frontiers, Ramiel grew to possess formidable strength which he used to join a mercenary company when he reached adulthood. Not much is known of Ramiel’s time with the mercenary company, but after his departure he began his current journey for redemption. Nobody truly knows what caused him to begin his intense adventure to redeem his soul but this journey for salvation eventually led him to an invitation to the Exemplars. Ramiel is currently the Exemplar stationed in Anatha.
