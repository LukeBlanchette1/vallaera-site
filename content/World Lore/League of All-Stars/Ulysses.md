$\quad$Ulysses was born in the Safazar Sultanate of Kalistis. Even as a child Ulysses was known to be reserved, more apt to silently observe than directly interact with the people around him. As a child he was revealed to be a chosen of Drana, the goddess of the moon. This forced him to enter the clergy of Drana. He eventually came into his powers and was able to see the constellations traced in the stars, and even see the “constellations” of people’s souls. Eventually, Ulysses grew bored of life as a priest and wanted to actively go out and help people directly. Ulysses then used his considerable god given talents to heal the sick and perform minor miracles for the people of Kalistis. Eventually, he gained enough notoriety to join the ranks of The Exemplars. Ulysses is currently the Exemplar stationed in Kalistis.
