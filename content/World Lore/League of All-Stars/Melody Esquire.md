$\quad$Melody Esquire was once a noble of The Grand Coast in Paladis. She was trained in the best bard colleges in Paladis and had a natural talent for oration. She had a reputation for being able to argue almost anything. She was a favorite in her father’s court and had a bright future ahead of her. When she grew older, she decided to leave her family behind and take on adventuring. She used her musical talents to make money on the road and used the excess to help the villagers around her. Through her charity, the town was able to improve itself and evolve, eventually becoming a center for local trade. This development touched Melody’s heart and it was then that she decided to help as many people as she could. She honed her magical abilities even more and became something of a local celebrity, eventually catching Orpheus’ eye. Orpheus invited her to the All-Star League and she graciously accepted. Now a global celebrity, she kicked her heroics into highgear and gained even more fans. Her talents then got her an invitation into the Exemplars where she now resides. She is the Exemplar currently stationed in Paladis.
