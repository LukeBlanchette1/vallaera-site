# Big Picture

What's the main plot? The Swords have found a group known as the [[Triad]], comprised of [[Mindrak]], [[Zinfir]], and [[Malakar]].

Each member of the Triad represents a faction fighting in Anatha right now. These factions, while they may seem unrelated at first, are working together towards a common goal. They want to revive [[Vance]] and bring the Triad back into power.

Mindrak's faction consists of the [[Mindrak Dynasty]], the [[Assimilists]], and the members of [[Unit 82]].

Malakar's faction consists of the [[Hollows]] and groups like the [[Lilac Troupe]].

Zinfir's faction consists of the [[Myrkhans]], such as [[Kinthar]].
