$\quad$While on the sea, if you see the water darken and the sky turn crimson, your life is already forfeit. The captain of The Dread Fleet has seen you, and he has marked you for death. No soul has ever escaped and none ever will. According to legend, Zeldrak is a powerful lich that commands his fleet of undead horrors. Sailors pray to Lantos for safety from Zeldrak before they depart the various ports of Vallaera but their prayers go unanswered. No one has ever survived an encounter with Zeldrak or his Dread Fleet. Not even The League of All-Stars have been successful in their attempts to free the world of Zeldrak’s terror. Zeldrak remains, just below the waves of Vallaera, looking for his next victim. There doesn’t seem to be any pattern for Zeldrak’s attacks. Whenever people sail the sea, they always know it may very well be their final voyage.
