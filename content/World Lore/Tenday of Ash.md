The Tenday of Ash was the ten days spanning the awakening of [[Drokai]] and [[Castellia]] to the day of their deaths, known as the [[Day of Deliverance]]. All of the [[Founders]] were engaged on missions to defeat the demigods during this period.

The first day was when the massive supervolcano under the continent of [[Drokai]] erupted due to the actions of the [[Rekindled]] drow destroying the [[Saints]] legendary relics. This eruption led to Drokai, the god, being released from his prison. This also released Castellia from her continent of present day [[Castkia]] as well. 

Ash, lava, and flaming debris were sent all throughout the world, blacking out the sun and destroying much of the world. Worse still, Drokai was attempting to wipe out all sentient life, and Castellia was planning on summoning the [[Demon Lords]] to the Material Plane to take control of it. This led to the world leaders holding a world summit on [[Tau Ma'at]] and the most famous heroes of the realm creating a hasty alliance, the [[Founders]], to defeat the two demigods.

The teams consisted of [[Red Team]] and [[Blue Team]].

Castellia was gathering high concentrations of people in [[Tavra]] to use as an offering for the Demon Lords she was summoning, and Drokai was opening the [[Leylines]] of the world in order to give himself the magical strength needed to eradicate all sentient life from the world, as he saw sentience as the inherent cause of all evil in the world.

The culmination of the fighting was when Blue Team went to Castkia to fight Drokai and Red Team went to the Heart of Tavra to fight Castellia and her demon army. By sheer grit, both teams prevailed and saved the world from certain doom. This day was named the [[Day of Deliverance]] and is a worldwide celebration.
