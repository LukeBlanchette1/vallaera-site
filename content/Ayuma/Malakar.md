Malakar was a powerful lich from [[Ayuma]]. He was born in 966 BC, being 479 years old during the events of the [[Tenday of Ash]]. He gained possession of the [[Soulreaper's Requiem]] in 352 AD, and dominated a large portion of [[Tavra]]. His rise to power led to a devastating duel between him and [[Meteor Mouskovitz]]. Malakar was killed in their showdown and Meteor Mouskovitz took the Soulreaper's Reqiuem to safeguard it.
