Enzio Kerrigan was the founder of [[The League of All-Stars]]. He was a member of the    [[Shadowsingers]], [[The League of Friendship]] and [[Blue Team]] of the [[Founders]]. He, along with the other All-Stars, fought against [[Drokai]] in the [[Tenday of Ash]]. He married [[Relena Kerrigan]] and had one son, [[Pierre Kerrigan]].

He was the head of The League of All-Stars until he retired and named [[Orpheus]] his successor.
