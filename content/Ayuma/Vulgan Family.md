The largest and most influential mafia in Valsonia.

The Vulgan family has been around for centuries. They have terrorized the Palm and the Royal Circle since their inception. Their member count is unknown but it is suggested by the King that they have dwindled, explaining the lack of action done in the past few years. Denounced by every ruler since [[Gravil the Impaler]] in the late 900’s. It is unknown to the public how long the family has been in Valsonia, but the earliest documentation of their existence is from a letter threatening the family from Gravil. Currently the Vulgan Family’s reputation and status is split as you’ll get vastly different opinions depending on where in Valsonia you ask. In the Palm you may be told they are anything from lowly criminals to the most dangerous terrorists. In the Wrist the people may tell you to simply be careful or even that they are the Wrist’s savior, their Saint who will save them from their depths of poverty. The current head of the family is [[Vollan Vulgan]], who is commonly referred to as ‘The Saint of the Wrist’ by residents of The Wrist.
