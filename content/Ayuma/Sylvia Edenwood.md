Sylvia is a member of [[The League of All-Stars]] and one of the last remaining members of [[The League of Friendship]]. After the events of the [[Tenday of Ash]], she returned to being a queen in her kingdom of [[Edril]]. She brought The Forests of Edril into a golden age unparalleled in its history. She was said to be a blur on the battlefield that could almost instantly turn her enemies into a red mist. None dared oppose her. She ruled for over 5 centuries, eventually retiring from ruling due to boredom. She granted rulership to her daughter, [[Sylvia Edenwood II]], who now rules.
