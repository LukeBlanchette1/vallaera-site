The Grim Territory of Fellmoor is split between the scattered forests of its north and the snow peaked mountains of its south. Fellmoor is known for its dark rock cliffs and the muted color of its woods. The minerals in the soil of Fellmoor has led to the flora gaining a more saturated hue. The Grim Territory of Fellmoor is known for its unique polearms such as halberds, pikes, and spears. This tradition started with the Fellmoor’s most famous rulers, [[Gavril the Impaler]] who had a proclivity for putting his enemies heads on pikes. The current ruler of Fellmoor is [[Domnitor Nicolai Vladovostok IV]].
