Valsonia is a city in [[Fellmoor]] in [[Ayuma]].

Valsonia is a city of the Arcana thanks to its 5 unique, native elemental flowers: Frostbloom (Cold), Fireheart (Fire), Strikeleaf (Lightning), Snapleaf (Thunder), and Purelight (Radiant). Each one of these flowers grows atop one of the Five Fingers. They are what give the magic artisans the ability to create the variety of magic items that are sold across the globe and known for not just their uses, but their presentation.

It is ruled by King [[Setral Purewater]]. It is also home to the [[Vulgan Family]].
