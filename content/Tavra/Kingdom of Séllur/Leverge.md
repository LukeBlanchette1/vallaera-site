> *"Wealth From the Waves"*
# General
The House that governs the most maritime trade in the empire. It controls the most profitable ports of Séllur. Their family has a sorcerous strain due to their fey ancestry. This fey bloodline is known for granting some members of the house psionic abilities. 

They rule from Chamtreu, "The Lavender Port" which is one of the most prosperous cities in [[Séllur]]. 

The late Duke Vestelier Leverge died at sea around 6 months ago. This left his young daughter, [[Ermesinde Leverge]] to assume his duties and inherit the [[Dawn Counties]].

Members of House Leverge can be identified from their white, purple, pink, or platinum blonde hair, their Fey lineage, and their gentle demeanor. They fly a coral pink banner with a teal Iridescent Fin upon it
# Notable Members
**Duchess Ermesinde Leverge**
*For more information see [[Ermesinde Leverge]]

**Councillor Bertran Leverge**
*For more infromation see [[Primrose Council]]*
- Bertran Leverge serves as House Leverge's representative on the Primrose Council. With the recent turmoil embroiling House Leverge, the influence he has garnered at his position has made him a bigger player in the internal politics of House Leverge than he was before. He is also well known for his business acumen and involvement in trade across the Dawn Counties.

**Count André Leverge**
- Count André Leverge of the Glittering Bay is the younger brother of the late Duke Vestelier and one of the other claimants to the title of Duke. There is a sizeable faction of the Dawn Counties that believe he should have been named Duke instead of Ermesinde although he has not publicly said anything.

**Great Mother Iania**
- Great Mother Iania is the mother of the late Duke Vestelier, Count André, and Count Florian, making her Ermesdine's grandmother. Great Mother Iania is understood to be the one really keeping things together for Ermesinde during her reign. 

**Lady Vana**
*For more information see [[Fleurier Order]]* 
- Lady Vana is a cousin of the major Leverge rulers but is prominent due to her role in the Fleurier Order. She is known to be a favorite of Duchess Ermesinde which has given her more influence across House Leverge and the Dawn Counties.
