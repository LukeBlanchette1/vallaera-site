Alphonse Destrier is Antonin's wartime friend. Alphonse was considered insane by all of the units he was a part of due to his ranting and ravings about spirits and how they are speaking to him. Alphonse holds a deep disdain for [[Séllur]]'s noble hierarchy which was exacerbated by his forced service in the rebellion. Beyond his apparent insanity, Alphonse is a talented necromancer. He is capable of impressive necromantic feats despite no apparent formal education.

His whereabouts after the [[Moondrop Rebellion]] are still unknown but he contacted Antonin to track down [[Midinet|Arlette Midinet]]. What she can contribute to his plans is unknown.
