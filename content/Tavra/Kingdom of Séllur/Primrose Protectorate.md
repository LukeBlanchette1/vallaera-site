The Primrose Protectorate is a somewhat controversial law being proposed in the Primrose Council. Traditionally laws under deliberation are not common knowledge but the Protectorate is the exception to this rule.

The Primrose Protectorate proposes that Fleurier Commander [[Henri Marleau]] become *Lord Protector* of Séllur. The exact powers of the Lord Protector are unclear to those not on the [[Primrose COuncil|Council]] but the common understanding is that the Lord Protector would serve some executive role in the Séllurian government now that [[Josette Jacquemond I|Queen Josette]] is asleep.
