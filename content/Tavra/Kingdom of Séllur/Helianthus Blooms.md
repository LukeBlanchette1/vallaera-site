*"When mixed together the three blooms create a potent elixir that can cure anything, or at least the limit has never been documented..."*

The Helianthus Blooms are the three flowers that contain incredibly potent healing potential and, when combined, are said to be able to cure anything. They only grow in Séllur and the Sacred Land of Gainier. 

They require a concentration of magic and a sort of ritual to actually blossom. They have immense healing capabilities individually but when ground together they can create an elixir that is capable of curing anything: **The Panacea**. They have not been sought out or even used in centuries and the actual rituals have long since been forgotten. The last recorded use was in 381 when the [[Blooming Blades|Archmage Quincy Gainier]] used them to cure *Long Day's Rot*, and incurable disease that afflicts the half-drow half-elves. 

[[Mourning Three|Lanstraya]] gave the Firestarters some information about the blooms. She recommended researching more or speaking to her sisters who may have more precise information:

**Akai Bloom**
- Historically native to the **Foxhold Woodlands**. It relies on deep concentrations of Transmutation magic to grow and blooms with a Druidic Ritual called **"The Petrichor Rites"**
- It is a beautiful irridescent orange and stories say it pulses with life and vitality.
- The last one is in possession of the Firestarters 

**Messai Bloom**
- Historically native to the **Azure Hills** of Séllur and the **Artash Meadow** in the Sacred Lands. Other details of it are unknown to the Firestarters and require further research.

**Pollai Blooms**
- Historically native to the **Silvermorn Forest** of Séllur. Other details of it are unknown to the Firestarters and require further research.
