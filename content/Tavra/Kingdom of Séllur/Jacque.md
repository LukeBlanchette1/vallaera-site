# Overview
Known as "the Star that Became Knight" Jacque is the legendary her of the [[Petaled Revolution]] and founder of the [[Jacquemond]] dynasty. His youth is largely unknown with the first records of him appearing in multiple reports of criminal activity across the [[Thescryan Empire]]. First hand accounts of him tell of his talent as a knight and powerful personality. It is hard to discern what stories or accounts of Jacque are fictional, embellished, or entirely true. At this point he is as much myth as he was man. 

During the chaos of [[Malakar]]'s empire clashing with the Thescryan Empire and [[The Hierarch]], Jacque called upon some of the most powerful people of the realms to form the [[Blooming Blades]]. The Blades seized their opportunity to strike at the weakened empire in the Petaled Revolution and successfully overthrew the Hierarch.
