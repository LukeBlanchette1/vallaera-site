# Overview
The Moondrop Rebellion started in the year 609 until the Treaty of Sunsrest ended it in 614. It began with a small group of Lunéria Worshippers, allegedly being backed by The [[Sacred Lands of Gainier]], intended to remove [[Séllurian Pantheon|Sélluria]] churches and worshippers from the West because they were historically only worshippers of Drana. Famously, House [[Coucher]] converted to worshipping Sélluria when they joined the kingdom. A large part of this conflict was with [[Kynric Whitefin]], who was the governor of the [[Darkwave Bay]]. Kynric led the rebellion has become a sort of martyr figure for the worshippers of [[Séllurian Pantheon|Lunéria]].

Rebellion between worshippers of Lunéria and the Kingdom's forces that hold Sélluria in the highest esteem. Incredibly bloody and reached across [[Séllur]] although it was focused in the South-West in the [[Dusk Counties]]. The Rebellion highlighted harsh divides that were simmering under the surface. The result of the Rebellion was the removal of religious spaces dedicated to Drana and forbid the [[Houses of Séllur]] from worshipping [[Séllurian Pantheon|Lunéria]]. 

# Cultural Impact
The Moondrop Rebellion was fought by Séllur's young and able. During this time, many of the young nobles rubbed shoulders with the subaltern citizens of Séllur in a more intimate way than ever before. This shaped and changed the next generation of Séllurian nobility, causing them to have a profoundly different attitude and understanding of their place in Séllur.

# Heroes
**Sir Saul Midinet**
*For more information see [[Primrose Council]]*
 Interim Commander of the [[Fleurier Order]] during the Rebellion after Sir Valoric was killed in action. Saul was largely respected before but his ability to quickly adapt to his new role and command Séllur's forces to victory catapulted him to a greater level of fame. Although he has since retired, he still serves on the Primrose Council and is active in Séllurian politics. 

**Sir Henri [[Marleau]]**
Sir Henri was the one to slay Kynric at the Battle of Blackcoast and end the rebellion for good. He gained immense renown as one of the most formidable knights Séllur had to offer. Survivors say he crashed into Kynric's forces with no fear and came back covered in both his own blood and theirs. He was named the next commander of the Fleurier Oder shortly after the Treaty of Sunsrest with unanimous approval. 

**Lady Innocence LaFoie**
Lady Innocence LaFoie was a young knight in the service of House Coucher during the rebellion. A sea elf herself, she was battled through skepticism and prejudice during her service. She was a key fighter in the Battle of Blackcoast and proved herself again and again during the rebellion. She was one of the first knights Henri chose to join the Fleurier Order when he became commander. 

**Sir Thane Cawdor** **"Twice Horned"**
Sir Thane Cawdor was a largely unknown figure who made a name for himself during the rebellion. He seemed to always be exactly where he was needed and used his powerful illusion magic to make seemingly impossible battles into easy victories. He was another member of the Fleurier Order that Henri chose himself.
