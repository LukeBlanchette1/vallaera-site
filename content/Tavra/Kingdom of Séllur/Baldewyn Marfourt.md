# Overview
Baldewyn Marfourt is the Court Chronicler for House [[Jacquemond]] and all of [[Séllur]]. He is the Head of the Royal Archives and a retired member of the [[Ivory Sun]]. Even for a half-elf he is incredibly old. He has been around since the [[Petaled Revolution]] where he got to personally know the [[Blooming Blades]] through his sister, Ena Marfourt. Being around for so long, he has chronicled most of the [[Houses of Séllur|Houses']] histories.

He has gone a bit mad in the last century, meaning that consultations with him have become rarer and rarer.
