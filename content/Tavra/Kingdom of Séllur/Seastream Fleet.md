# Overview
The Seastream Fleet is a fleet of pirates led by a notorious swashbuckler known as Captain Andreva, a hobgoblin pirate from the Archduchy of Zakurann. This fleet has recently taken to terrorizing the [[Vykesian Sea]], which has frequently interrupted the trade between [[Portsdam]] and [[Séllur]]. These pirate incursions have created even more chaos in Séllur but their sustained presence and focus on Séllur has many wondering if there is more at play than pirate greed...
