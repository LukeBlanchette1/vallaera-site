# Overview
The Sunset Coast is the western border of [[Séllur]] and located entirely in the [[Dawn Counties]]. The Sunset Coast is an incredibly popular tourist spot with resorts all along the coast. Many of the [[Leverge]] palaces are located along coast. Long ago the Sunset Coast had its own fae citizens that became House Leverge. 

Travel to the coast has decreased since [[Josette Jacquemond I|Josette]] has begun her slumber and the enriching sun has lost its luster.
