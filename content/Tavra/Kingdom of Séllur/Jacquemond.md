# General
House Jacquemond is the ruling house of [[Séllur]] and can trace back their lineage to Séllur's founder: [[Jacque]] "the Star Made Knight". House Jacquemond directly controls the [[Crownlands]] but rules every holding in Séllur. House Jacquemond is the most prestigious house in all of Séllur and are the fewest in number. Members of House Jacquemond has a strong grip on its members to ensure none of their actions cause instability in the realm.

Members of House Jacquemond can be identified by their golden to light brown hair, sun-kissed skin, and golden dress. They are largely [[Séllurian Species & Culture|Strunian]] human or even half-elven in more recent generations. They claim the golden sun as their symbol.
