# Overview
The Fleurier Order is the most prestigious order in [[Séllur]]. Rather than swearing an oath to one of the [[Houses of Séllur|Noble Houses]], The Fleurier Order swears an oath to the Kingdom itself. Members of the Fleurier Order are selected from amongst the most competent of the [[Seven Orders]] that dedicate themselves to a house. 

The Fleurier Order are seen as the successors of the [[Blooming Blades]] legacy, serving to protect the realm from any manner of threat it may face. With the recent slumber of Queen [[Josette Jacquemond I]] and the influx of creatures it has caused, the Fleurier Order has been stretched thin trying to deal with threats across Séllur at once. 

The Fleurier Order is led by a Commander, traditionally from the Houses of [[Jacquemond]], [[Midinet]], or [[Marleau]]. The 26th and current Commander is [[Primrose Council|Henri Marleau]] who has served in this capacity for 9 years. 

The Fleurier Order are well known throughout Séllur for their exploits and heroic deeds. Many of the Order were lost during the [[Moondrop Rebellion]] so the majority of the current knights are young and hand chosen by Henri. The order is highly selective so there are often less than 20 members of the order with the current count being 13.
