# Overview
First Duke of the [[Crownlands]] and one of the major forces in keeping House [[Jacquemond]] afloat while Queen [[Josette Jacquemond I|Josette]] is out of the picture. Duncan Darby is originally from [[Portsdam]], hailing from one of their more powerful families. He is the father of Prince-Regent [[Hubert Jacquemond|Hubert]] and Princess Honora.

Despite not being from Séllur he is generally well liked, especially in the Crownlands. Now in his 50s he has held the position of First Duke for close to 25 years. He has won the Séllurian people over with his charisma and willingness to adopt Séllurian tradition and culture. 

His marriage to Josette has opened [[Séllur]] to more trade with Portsdam, and more access to the goods that Portsdam's many colonies provide. This has caused a rise in more exotic goods in Séllur and improved ability to export Séllurian goods abroad.
