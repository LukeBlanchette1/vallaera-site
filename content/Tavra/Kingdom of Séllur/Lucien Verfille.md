# Overview
Lucien Verfille is the second son of [[Esclamonde Verfille|Count Esclamonde]] of [[Verfille|House Verfille]] and the First Councillor of the [[Primrose Council]]. Lucien came to prominence following his service in the [[Moondrop Rebellion]] where he served in the Arcanist Battalion. He was named First Councillor following the death of theFirst Councillor, Duke Vestelier [[Leverge]] 

Even though he is only in his 30s, he is considered one of the most influential members of House Verfille. His arcanic disposition  more "diplomatic" personality have caused some public friction between him an his own house. H

As First Councillor, he is also the governor and administrator of [[New Sélluria]] which has taken strides in its development under his stewardship. He has taken much of its administration into his own hands now that [[Josette Jacquemond I|Queen Josette]] is slumbering.
