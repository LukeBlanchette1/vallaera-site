# Overview
Vivienne is the Duchess of the [[Day Counties]] and leader of House [[Midinet]]. She has ruled for many years and led the [[Day Counties]] with honor. She is popular in the Day Counties and has a strong hold on the members of House Midinet. She has 8 children, Count Claudin, Count Ambrose, and Countess Vivienne "the Lesser" are only 3 of her kids. Her favorite is the youngest, Arlette, who has recently gone missing without a trace. House Midinet blames House Coucher but Coucher has denied it.

Her younger brother [[Primrose Council|Sir Saul Midinet]] was the commander of the [[Fleurier Order]] for many years before his retirement and subsequent position on the Primrose Council as House Midinet's representative.
