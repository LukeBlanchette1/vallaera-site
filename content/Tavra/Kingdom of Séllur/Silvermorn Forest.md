# Overview
The Silvermorn Forest dominates the north-eastern reaches of [[Séllur]]. The Forest lies entirely in the [[Crownlands]] and within the lands of [[Lansois|House Lansois]]. The Silvermorn Forest is notably colder than the rest of the realm, having light snow most of the year. The forest is also has the highest concentration of both fae and fae magic. The forest is filled with ruins believed to be from an ancient Fae kingdom. 

The Silvermorn Forest is home to many isolated [[Séllurian Species & Culture|Fae]] communites. The Silvermorn Fae include Fairies, Changelings, Eladrin, and many others. House Lansois allows them a large degree of autonomy in exchange for occasional assistance and subervience.

# Bestiary
Many creatures of the forest have adapted and been influenced by the pervasive fae magic. Wild fae roam the forest, attacking those that they come across. There are many accounts of rare creatures like unicorns, displacer beasts, and even old tales of jabberwockies that stalk the forest late at night.
