# Overview
The second child of [[Josette Jacquemond I]] and [[Duncan Darby]], a member of House [[Jacquemond]].

She is currently married to Leksandre, the son of [[Vivienne Midinet|Duchess Vivienne]] of House [[Midinet]] which solidified a powerful alliance between the two houses. She has two children with Leksandre. 

She was the expected heir to the throne and seemingly the chosen successor of Josette. She had been groomed for the position, even marrying Leksandre to ensure an easy transfer of power. However, this was called into question when her eldest brother, [[Hubert Jacquemond]] was named regent in her place. With the votes of the [[Primrose Council]] being anonymous to the general public, very few people understand the rationale behind this decision. 

Since Hubert's election, Honora has been largely absent from the public eye, only appearing at events that require her attendance.
