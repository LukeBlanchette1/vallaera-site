*"I have never known someone so honorable. There is none closer to Jacque than Henri. Stars and flames are not as different as we think.*
*- Honor Jacquemond*

Henri [[Marleau]] is the current commander of the [[Fleurier Order]] and the greatest knight of his generation. He bears great draconic wings, one of the gifts of House Marleau that marks him for greatness. He sits on the [[Primrose Council]] as the Fleurier Commander but lacks political tact. The [[Primrose Protectorate]] is the only proposition that he has shown enthusiastic support for.

Henri distinguished himself during the [[Moondrop Rebellion]] where he slew [[Kynric Whitefin]] himself. This accomplishment saw him be elected as the next Commander of the Fleurier Order unanimously. 

Henri is known as Séllur's most eligible bachelor. Despite entering his 30s, he has never publicly courted or been with anyone. Ladies from across the [[Houses of Séllur|Houses]] have tried to woo him but none have ever succeeded. 

He is the son of Sir Ordis Vathric, a lowborn knight, and Lady Maura Marleau. Count [[Orderic Marleau]] is Henri's uncle and [[Primrose Council|Councillor Daliah Marleau]] is his older sister. He wields the legendary swords *Eskathielle* and *Krth'Ik*. *Eskathielle* was inherited from his father and *Krth'Ik* is the dynastic sword of House Marleau, originally wielded by [[Blooming Blades|Sir Thaselot Marleau]] of the Blooming Blades.
