# General
House Midinet is one of the [[Houses of Séllur|Greater Houses]] in Séllur, ruling as Dukes of the [[Day Counties]]. Founded by [[Blooming Blades|Marian]], one of the original Blooming Blades, they hold the city of Marin's Hall as their capital. They are recognized for their martial might and fostering of [[The Blanket]]. Their [[Seven Orders|Knights of the Great Harvest]] are acclaimed for their dominant cavalry. The current head of House Midinet is Duchess [[Vivienne Midinet]], who has led the House for 28 prosperous years.

# Midinet and Coucher Rivalry
House Midinet has a storied rivalry with House [[Coucher]] dating back centuries with Lady Marian and Sir Dorian. Their rivalry reached critical mass around 50 years ago when the [[Dusk Counties]] were being conquered by [[Séllur]]. Duke [[Drakith Coucher|Drakith's]] brother Vykith, the Archduke of the Sovereign Dusk Counties, was captured by Duke Arnaud of the Day Counties, grandfather of Duchess Vivienne. Duke Arnaud chose to guard Vykith personally which was unusual for a Duke. Nobody knows exactly what transpired between the two, but they were both found dead in that dungeon. Both bodies were charred beyond recognition. 

Both houses blamed the other for dishonorably slaying their kin. The Midinet felt that Vykith could only have done this through backstabbing the honorable Duke Arnaud and using dark magic to do it. House Coucher believes that Duke Arnaud attacked and killed the imprisoned Vykith, causing Vykith to try whatever he could to retaliate. 

Whatever the truth of the situation is, a century of blood feuds cannot be solved overnight. Recent events seem to have stoked the embers of this rivalry, and with the [[Josette Jacquemond I|Queen]] slumbering and the court in turmoil, the two houses seem to have no mediator.
