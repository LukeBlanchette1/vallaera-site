# Overview
The Southern counties of [[Séllur]], ruled by House [[Midinet]] from their capital of Marinhall. The Day Counties are by far the most populous in all of Séllur. The Day Counties produce most of Séllur's food which means that roads through the Day Counties are incredibly well kept. The population of the Day Counties is largely comprised of [[Séllurian Species & Culture|Fedelven]] farmers, especially throughout the countryside. The cities and larger coastal settlements are home to more diverse communities. 
# Geography
The Day Counties are dominated by a large expanse of farmland known as [[The Blanket]] which they take great pride in. The Day Counties have a predictable warm climate with very hot summers and wet winters. The almost mechanical precipitation has helped the agriculture of the Day Counties along with the constant sunlight being blessed by the [[Soleil]], although the harvest has diminished without the Soleil's rays.
# Counties
**Marinhall**
*For more information see [[Vivienne Midinet]]*
The County of Marinhall is ruled by Duchess Vivienne Midinet and is the traditional title of House Midinet's leader. The county of Marinhall is named after its capital, the city of Marinhall which was founded by [[Blooming Blades|Marian Midinet]], the founder of House Midinet. Marinhall is the largest county in Séllur. Marinhall is responsible for over half of the Day Counties' produce.

**Austenhalt**
The County of Austenhalt is ruled by Count Claudin, the eldest son of Duchess Vivienne. Austenhalt is the center of the Day Counties' maritime trade. Austenhalt is ruled from Crestenhall, home of the prestigious Maghausen Academy.

**Goldmane**
The County of Goldmane is ruled by Count Ambrose, the second eldest son of Duchess Vivienne. Goldmane is ruled from Belvedere, the southernmost major city in Séllur. Many people searching for a place to vacation on the Vykessian Sea look to resorts in Goldmane.

**Steelhold**
The County of Steelhold is ruled by Countess Vivienne "the Lesser," the eldest daughter of Duchess Vivienne. Castelfeld is the capital of Steelhold, home to one of the oldest [[Séllurian Species & Culture|Lantid]] Elf ruins. The city of Castelfeld was built with the ruins long ago, leading to many archaeological discoveries even today.
