# Overview
The Foxhold Woodlands is the largest forest in [[Séllur]], spanning 7 counties. The heart of the woodlands lies in the counties of [[Crownlands|Foxhold, Monteu, and Villefort]]. The woodlands are also considered the ancestral home of House [[Verfille]] with [[Rothmund's Hills]] lying within the vast forest.

Old legend tells of a place deep within the woodland known only as The Heart of Foxhold. The Heart is said to be a place of intense magic where any wish can be granted and any cruse broken. Many have ventured to locate it but none have ever succeeded. 
# Demographics & Geography
The Foxhold Woodlands is very different from [[Silvermorn Forest]], Séllur's other large swath of woodland. The Foxhold Woodland is temperate and home to many beasts and their own fey communities. The fey of the Foxhold Woodlands include Red Caps, [[Séllurian Species & Culture|Firbolg]], Satyr, Formorians, and many others. 

The Woodlands are twisting and need an experienced guide to navigate with any level of efficiency. The many villages that lie within the woodlands talk about the woodland as if it has its own personality and dark sense of humor. The citizens of the Woodlands share a similar grim humor due to their close proximity with death.

The Foxhold Woodlands are known for their brigands and rogues who roam the dense Woodland looking for lost travelers or merchant's cargo. The various counts of the Woodland have sent their [[Seven Orders|knights]], but a substantial decrease in theft has not materialized.

# Bestiary
Many monsters are said to roam the woodlands although much less are actually ever seen. Rumors of dragons, umber hulks, and purple worms abound but travelers are much more likely to encounter an owl bear or boggle.
