# Overview
The Western coast of [[Séllur]], ruled by House [[Leverge]]. The Dawn Counties compromise the majority of Séllur's maritime trade and is home to Séllur's greatest port and Dawn Counties capital, Chamtreu. The Dawn Counties are a prime destination for those hoping to become merchants or join an artisan's guild. 
# Geography
The Dawn Counties are known to be gentle and bountiful. The beaches of the [[Sunset Coast ]]have the softest sand in the kingdom, and the people of the Dawn Counties are considered to be the kindest in Séllur. 
# Counties
**Chamtreu**
*For more information see [[Ermesinde Leverge]]*
The county of Chamtreu is the capital of the Dawn Counties and its namesake city is by far the Duchy's largest. Ermesinde Leverge is the Countess of Chamtreu and Duchess of the Dawn Counties making her the de facto leader of House Leverge. 

**Flutterstem**
The county of Flutterstem is ruled from Reneu's Rest. Count Toulouse Leverge rules Flutterstem, a cousin of Duchess Ermesinde. 

**The Cloud Coast**
The county of the Cloud Coast is ruled from Turnfin Beach. The County of the Cloud Coast is ruled by Count Florian Leverge, uncle of Duchess Ermesinde and father of Count Toulouse. 

**The Glittering Bay**
The county of the Glittering Bay is ruled by Port Danfroth. The Glittering Bay is ruled by Count André Leverge, uncle of Duchess Ermesinde.
