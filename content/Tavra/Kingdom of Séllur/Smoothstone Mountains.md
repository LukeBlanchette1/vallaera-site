# Overview
The Smoothstone Mountains lie in both the [[Crownlands]] and [[Dusk Counties]]. The mountains are mineral rich and sloping rather than the jagged peaks of [[Rothmund's Hills]]. The Smoothstone Mountains were once the home of the orcish House Votarre which has since died out.
# Geography & Demographics
The Smoothstone Mountains still have the highest [[Séllurian Species & Culture|Orcish]] population in Séllur. The communities on the Smoothstone Mountains make their living as trappers and miners. [[Séllurian Guilds & Organizations|The New Path]] is based in the Smoothstone Mountains and has a good hold on its communities.
