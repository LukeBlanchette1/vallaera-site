# Overview
The Blanket is the vast farmlands that span the [[Day Counties]] and produces the majority of [[Séllur|Séllur's]] produce. The Blanket is a staple cultural icon for the Day Counties and [[Séllurian Species & Culture|Fedelven]] humans who believe they sprouted from the Blanket like the grain they harvest. 

One of the most important roles of the [[Seven Orders|Great Harvest Order]] is to protect The Blanket and assist in its great harvest before Prosper Day.
