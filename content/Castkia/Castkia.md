Out of the [[Continents]], Castkia is the largely unexplored continent at the North Pole of Vallaera. Many nations across the world have colonies on the continent to extract its valuable natural resources. Castkia is the only place in the world to contain [[Coldsteel]], a uniquely durable metal. Castkia is said to be home to a variety of monsters but what lies in the Frozen Frontier is unknown. Many expeditions have been sent to explore what lies to the far north, but not all of them return.
