The Efferant Wastes refers to the massive expanse of desert that dominates northern [[Kalistis]]. The Wastes is home to its own unique traditions and people. The Wastes is the largest desert in all of Vallaera and home to some of its most hostile wildlife. 

In ancient tradition, The Wastes are said to have their own personality called [[The Spirit of the Wastes]]. Today, This tradition lives on today through the druids of [[The Topaz Circle]]. 

The Efferant Wastes lend their name to the [[Effranian Empire]], who was named for the vast desert.
