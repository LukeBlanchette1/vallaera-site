The [[Topaz Circle]] is a druidic faction in [[Kalistis]]. They are targeted for potentially being the cause of [[The Five Plagues]], particularly by the [[Sacrosanct]]. They are led by the archdruid [[Ishtar]], and there are three leaders below her, the [[Hierophants]].
