A Khenra who loses their twin is called a Unkhara (meaning incomplete in Khenra). This is considered a tragic fate. An Unkhara will always be seen as not quite complete, never fully able to realize their potential. Unkhara are typically more reckless and violent as a result of their loss. They tend to be deeply mistrusting and aggressive, although some Unkhara are exceptions. Oftentimes an Unkhara will leave Khenra society and find a life somewhere further away. An Unkhara can complete the [[Trials of Khufara]], although this is much harder for them, as their spiritual connection will be significantly more challenging to form.
