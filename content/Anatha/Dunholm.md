Dunholm is located in [[Thalrune]] and is part of [[The Holt]].

**Dunholm** is full of sprawling forests with relatively small and weak creatures. There are many small towns, goblin camps, wild beasts, and more here. It is a common stomping grounds for fledgeling adventurers. Fey are found around this area, although this is rare. The [[Verdant Covenant]] mainly resides within Dunholm.
