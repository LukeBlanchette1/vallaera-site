Hearthfall is located in [[Thalrune]] and is part of [[The Moor]].

**Hearthfall** is the most populous of the Keeps, bigger even than Elderspire. It is mostly rolling fields with groves sprinkled throughout the Keep, and many towns are scattered throughout it. People here are very friendly and have a good sense of hospitality. There are many crop fields and cattle farms along the way.
