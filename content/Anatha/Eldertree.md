The Eldertree is located in [[Dunholm]]. It is where the [[Verdant Covenant]] resides. It is the product of centuries of druidic cultivation of a single tree, allowing it to grow beyond a single plant and become an aspect of all of nature around it. It became a spiritual being that druids could commune with. Many members of the Verdant Covenant are connected to the Eldertree, and they feel its pain themselves. WHe
