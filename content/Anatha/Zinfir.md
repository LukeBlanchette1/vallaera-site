Zinfir was a [[Myrkhans|myrkhan]] from the lost nation of [[Vance]]. He was an anomaly amonst myrkhans. He had a unique genetic trait that prevented his body from initiating cell division, which meant he could not divide and reproduce. However, this also meant he perpetually retained twice the number of organs as a normal creature. This gave him not only overwhelming physical prowess, but also incredible intellect. Two brains gave him immense computational power, which led to him becoming the best tactician and general in [[Vallaera]]. 

Zinfir conquered most of [[Anatha]], effortlessly overwhelming entire nations with his myrkhan soldiers and growing military force. Despite resistance from across the continent, and even some clashing with The League of All-Stars, Zinfir was able to conquer all of Anatha, and just needed the Djerith Highlands to complete his domination. Attacking the hills and mountains of the Highlands was dangerous, especially with the powerful giant armies bearing down on Zinfir’s army. Despite these obstacles, Zinfir’s forces were able to push the Djerith army back to their capital of Stonerest nestled in the mountains. Here is where the League of All-Stars truly stepped in and made their stand. They were barely able to stop Zinfir from taking the bastion but were unable to truly make a dent in Zinfir’s forces in the process. Zinfir prepared his troops for one last offensive while the All-Stars attempted to rest and breached the walls of the city. 

The battle was vicious and went either way for a while. It was then that Enzio Kerrigan used his final Wish, the most powerful spell in Vallaera, to bring Zinfir down. This battle is known as the [[Siege of Stonerest]]. This battle was the last [[Enzio Kerrigan]] would fight in, as he stepped down from leading the All-Stars and passed the mantle off to [[Orpheus]],

After his death, Zinfir's body was taken by myrkhans loyal to him. They buried him in secret, in a location unbeknownst to the rest of the world, so that their leader could have a proper burial. Despite centuries of treasure hunters searching deep under Anatha for his tomb, none have found it. It is believed Zinfir was buried with his legendary weapon, [[Martyred Lilies]].
