Lord Molstra rules over [[Ravenholt]]. He is a gaunt, tired man fatigued from decades of dealing with constant monstrous dangers appearing in Ravenholt. He manages to hold the [[Keeps|Keep]] together despite all this. He is fast approaching the age to retire, and since he has no children, he must pass on the throne to someone else. However, his disdain of every option for successor has stopped him from stepping down. He is quicker to pick out someone's flaws than their gifts, and is known for being quite blunt.
