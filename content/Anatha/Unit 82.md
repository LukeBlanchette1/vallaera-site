Unit 82 is a rumored conspiracy about the Mindrak Dynasty. The conspiracy aims to explain the sudden and unexplained disappearance of 82 children from their cribs in one night. The children came from all across the Mindrak Dynasty, and grieving parents never found any trace of them. 

The Unit 82 conspiracy alleges the 82 infants were taken on Mindrak's orders and brought to an underground facility, where they were tortured and trained into killing machines loyal to Mindrak. It is said they were meant to be Mindrak's dark elite force, and that they were sent to lurk as sleeper agents in the surrounding countries.

The [[Swords of Shuss]] confirmed the existence of one Unit 82 member, [[Orion]].
