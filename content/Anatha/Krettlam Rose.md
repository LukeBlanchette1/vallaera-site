The Krettlam Rose is a crime syndicate primarily based in southern [[Anatha]], [[Ayuma]], and western [[Motu]], however they have contacts globally. The Rose deals in mostly contraband and hired gun roles, but doesn't deal drugs much. They can smuggle WMD's or illicit material across national borders, as well as mercenary killings, blackmail gathering, or evidence tampering. 

Members of the Krettlam Rose are often denotated by their thorny vine tattoo running up the side of their neck. The number of thorns and roses represents the number of jobs they've completed for the Rose. 

Nobody knows who runs the Krettlam Rose, and the syndicate gets exponentially more mysterious the closer you get to the top. However, urban legends describe [[The Grimm]] as the leader. This is unconfirmed.
