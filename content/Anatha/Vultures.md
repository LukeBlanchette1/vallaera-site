The Vultures are a [[Warbands|warband]] led by [[Balgor]], a Hollow. They mostly travel within the western portion of [[Dragonskeep]].

It was previously led by Garran Olso. One, day Balgor, the founder of the Vultures, a man who died 60 years prior, walked into camp. He claimed the Vultures as his own, and when Garran scoffed at him, Balgor murdered him. Now, Balgor runs the show.

The Vultures like to raid small towns, taking not just gold, but all sorts of food, tools, blankets, even doors and shutters. This is where they got the name Vultures - they pick everything clean, down to the bone, just as a vulture does. The towns they raid look like hollow ghost towns after they swoop in.
