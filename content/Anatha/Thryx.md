Thryx is an aarakocra from [[Eyris]]. He is a flight supremacist, believing that those that can fly on their own are the rightful denizens of the [[Sky Islands]]. He has since launched his own personal crusade against non-flying residents of [[Eyris]] and [[Aerithra]], killing those without wings and throwing their bodies off of the islands. Thryx's flying ability is unmatched amongst aarakocra, which is how he has evaded capture thus far. He strikes at night, attacking the homes of non-avian islanders. They live in fear of him, calling him the "Nightstalker".
