The Triad is an alliance between three powerful villains in the [[Founders]] era after [[Drokai]] and [[Castellia]] were defeated. The trio consisted of [[Mindrak]], [[Zinfir]], and [[Malakar]]. Together, they conquered all of [[Anatha]] under the empire of [[Vance]]. They were nigh unstoppable until the situation became so dire the [[Founders]] had to join the fight. 

In the [[Siege of Stonerest]], Zinfir was defeated, and Vance crumbled. Mindrak defected to the All-Stars and Malakar faded into hiding. Since then, the Triad has officially been vanquished.

In recent years, however, the lieutenants of the old Triad leaders have begun making moves. [[Kinthar]], the [[Hat Man]], and others made a resurgence in [[Thalrune]]. Whatever the reason, it appears that the Triad has returned in one form or another.
