The Unionists are staunch loyalists to their country. They argue that if no one stands up to Mindrak, then he will take over all of Anatha without so much as a fight. They are willing to die defending their home, and they refuse to capitulate to Mindrak’s provocations. They believe that life under Mindrak’s rule is not worth living, and they will defend their freedom to their last breath. They propose a firm stance that the disputed territory is Thalrunian and to be prepared to go to war with the Dynasty if it comes to it.
