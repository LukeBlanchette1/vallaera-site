Mindrak is an ancient blue dragon that rules with absolute power over the [[Mindrak Dynasty]]. He is exceptionally cunning, powerful, and dangerous. Mindrak is fluent in [[Eldertongue]]. 

Mindrak is widely thought to be the strongest being in [[Anatha]], stronger even than [[Ramiel Saturine]] the [[Exemplars|Exemplar]]. Because he is so strong, his nation dominates those around it. 

Mindrak is known for his long, elaborate, confusing schemes. He will plan steps decades in advance, and prefers to use his enemies' own minds against them. Mindrak revels in deception, confusion, and anxiety. Despite Mindrak's overwhelming draconic power being a key aspect of the Dynasty's strength, Mindrak has not personally had to fight in over a decade, due to his schemes success rates.
