The Artisan’s Guild is a loose conglomeration of craftsman from around the world. They have a chapter in almost every major city, the headquarters of The Artisan’s Guild can be found in Ayuma. The Artisan’s Guild ensures that prices on Artisanal goods and services remain fair and competitive. The Artisan’s Guild is known to fight for fair labor laws in nations, anti-discriminatory policies for employment, and help up and coming craftsmen get their start.
