> "Lady of the Night, Arbiter of Darkness, I call to you, hear my cry!"
>    *- Priestess Do, Disciple of Traniara*
 
The Sisters of the Crow is a faction of exclusively women dedicated to [[Traniara]]. They believe in the sanctity of death and a revulsion to undead. The faction was small prior to the emergence of [[Hollows]], but has grown into a well-revered faction since the Hollows began to wreak havoc. Their main goal is to hunt down Hollows and locate their origin.

Their symbol is a black crow. They travel the night, and each carry the blessing of their goddess.
