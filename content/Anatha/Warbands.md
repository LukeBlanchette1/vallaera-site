Warbands are groups of organized raiders that live in the northern highlands of [[Thalrune]]. The warbands raid towns throughout the highlands, pillaging their food supplies, weapons, and slaughtering the commoners before retreating back into the snowy peaks. 

There are three primary warbands:

The [[Vultures]]

The [[Marauders]]

The [[Reavers]]

The warbands have loose communcation between each other, but they never cooperate with one another.
