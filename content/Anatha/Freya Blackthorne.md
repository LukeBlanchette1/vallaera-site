Lady Blackthorne of [[Hearthfall]] is considered to be the epitome of Hearthfall's warm and welcoming reputation. Typically, women are forbidden to rule over a [[Keeps|Keep]], but Lady Blackthorne was so beloved by the public, that when her husband passed away, protests and riots broke out when it was announced that King [[Gofric Vaelen|Gofric]] was instituting a new Lord. The public outcry was so immense that the King capitulated, breaking the centuries-old precedent. Lady Blackthorne has resided over Hearthfall for the past 15 years as its sole ruler, and public opinion has not wavered in the slightest. Hearthfall has flourished under her rule and her popularity has spilled over into the other Keeps as well, earning her the title "The Great Lady".
