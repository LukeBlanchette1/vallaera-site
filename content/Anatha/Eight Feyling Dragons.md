The Eight Feyling dragons are a group of eight small, feytouched dragons. Each one is roughly the size of a fox. They all possess the ability to speak common, and are all considered tricksters. They have a wide array of wacky magical abilities, most of which they use to play pranks or games.

There are only eight Feyling dragons in existence. They are the byproduct of an Archfey's deal with eight dragons for each of their firstborn children. The Archfey raised the Feyling dragons in the Feywild alongside her own magic, which gave them their signature feylike personalities. The Feyling dragons can freely travel between the Material Plane and the Feywild. Most have left their mother's home and now are spread all across the world.
