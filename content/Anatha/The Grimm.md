The Grimm is a myth, with no real evidence to prove her existence or not. The stories say she is a doppelganger that secretly runs the crime syndicate, the [[Krettlam Rose]], from the shadows. She is described as a force of nature: her ability to perfectly replicate others is flawless, unparalleled even amongst doppelgangers. Some tales say she's the offspring of Ekrasil and a shapeshifting spirit, others say she's a [[Demon Lords|Demon Lord]] who found their way to the Material Plane.

Many political assassinations have been attributed to The Grimm, but nobody can say for certain. Often, a sudden disappearance or a mysterious death will have people whispering about The Grimm. However, others scoff at her existence, saying she's just another boogeyman, an urban legend parents tell their children at night.
