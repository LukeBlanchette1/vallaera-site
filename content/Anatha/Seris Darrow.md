Lord Seris Darrow is the ruler of [[Varghold]]. A heavily economics-driven leader, Lord Darrow prioritizes trade and wealth over most other aspects of governing. Foremost a businessman, Lord Darrow has made Varghold a highly profitable [[Keeps|Keep]]. However, critics of him say he is too easily swayed by gifts or donations from large businesses. Lord Darrow lives luxuriously with his wife and children in Varghold Proper.
