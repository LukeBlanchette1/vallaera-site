Lord Lunarbloom rules over [[Dunholm]]. He is a spry and eccentric fellow, sporting an odd collection of stuffed game, preserved flowers, wicker sculptures, and more. He is an avid hunter and often organizes large hunting parties with fellow nobles. Lord Lunarbloom doesn't take being a Lord too seriously. He does what needs to be done, and little more. Dunholm does well, not because of his leadership, but rather in spite of it. Dunholm is mostly comprised of small, isolated villages, so there isn't too much Lord Lunarbloom needs to be doing. Nevertheless, he keeps his citizens happy and is overall well liked.
