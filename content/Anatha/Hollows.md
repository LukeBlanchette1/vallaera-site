Hollows are the dead brought back to life. They walk around as they did in life, but something about them is wrong, unnatural. 

The Hollows began to appear two years ago. The first reported sighting of a Hollow was when an old baron from 1318 BC walked into [[Malan Gildhart]]'s castle and demanded that Lord Gildhart get off of his land. The baron was quickly executed.

Recent evidence uncovered by the [[Swords of Shuss]] indicates that the Hollows may be connected to the [[Soulreaper's Requiem]]. It also appears that the [[Hat Man]] is heavily involved in the Hollows appearing.
