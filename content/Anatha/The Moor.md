A common namesake for the region encompassing the eastern Keeps of [[Dragonskeep]], [[Elderspire]], [[Hearthfall]], and [[Varghold]]. All these [[Keeps]] have the same characteristic rolling fields and open landscapes. The Moor produces the majority of the food for Thalrune, and it is also considered more peaceful than the rest of Thalrune. 

The rest of [[Thalrune]] ([[Huldar's Reach]], [[Ravenholt]], and [[Dunholm]]) are collectively known as [[The Holt]].
