The Cloaks of Maka is a group of twelve archmages dedicated to the arcane arts around [[Vallaera]]. It was founded by Meteor Mouskovitz. Currently, there are only ten mages in the group, as the group has not fully recovered after the deaths caused by [[Corinth the Mad]]. Two of the current ten mages are residents of [[Anatha]], [[Velerion Lorestead]] and [[Wynna Tyvertrout]].
