The Reverent Duchess is the largest airship in Thalrune. It has ruby-red sails and a long blue stripe along its hull. It is currently owned by Lord [[Seris Darrow]] of [[Varghold]]. 

It was a gift from Lord [[Bymir Clearwater]] of [[Skymwan]] in [[Ayuma]] in exchange for Skymwan's exclusive access to many of Varghold's private ports.
