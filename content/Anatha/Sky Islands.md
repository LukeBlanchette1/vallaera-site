The Sky Islands are floating islands made of the floating debris that came off of the [[Crumbled Peaks]]. There are dozens, if not hundreds, of floating islands that drift around Anatha. Most are small and only inhabited by vegetation and wild animals, although some are known to have large cities on them. The [[Cloudborn]], a race of sky-dwelling people, are native to sky islands.
