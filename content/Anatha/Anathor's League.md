Anathor's League is a gladiator contest, not unlike the Super Bowl, that stretches across all of [[Anatha]], where contestants fight one another in various regions' [[Gladiator Bowls]]. The winner of each of these Bowls is invited to the [[Final Showdown]], held in an arena in a different ountry each year. Even getting to participate in a Bowl is a great honor, and to participate in the Final Showdown is considered something only legends can achieve. 

There are 16 Gladiator Bowls total in [[Anathor's League]]. They are:

The Peace Bowl, held in the [[Dozal Federation]]
The Violet Bowl, held in [[Springshade]]
The Rune Bowl, held in [[Thalrune]]
The Orion Bowl, held in [[Glimmerworth]]
The Shattered Bowl, held in [[Atha-Uruk]]
The King's Bowl, held in the [[Mindrak Dynasty]]
The Gem Bowl, held in Glimmerworth
The Silver Bowl, held in the [[High Republic]]
The Dust Bowl, held in the [[Djerith Highlands]]
The Relic Bowl, held in the Djerith Highlands
The Lost Bowl, held in the [[Chumbian Isles]]
The Blood Bowl, held in Atha-Uruk
The Pewter Bowl, held in Springshade
The Marble Bowl, held in Thalrune
The War Bowl, held in Atha-Uruk
The Sea Bowl, held in the Dozal Federation

The winner of the Final Showdown is granted the title "Anathor's Champion". The [[Storm Herald]] is currently Anathor's Champion.

If a contestant wins three Final Showdowns concurrently, they can challenge the previous Champion for their title. Lots of energy is buzzing around Anathor's League this year, as [[Kas]] sits at two concurrent Final Showdown wins. If he wins this year, he will face the Storm Herald for the throne.
