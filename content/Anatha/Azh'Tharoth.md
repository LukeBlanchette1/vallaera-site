Azh'Tharoth is considered to be the progenitor of dragons and the direct son of [[Kyros]], leader of the gods. Azh'Tharoth's scales are a kaleidoscope of colors that shimmer brightly. Azh'Tharoth is twice as large as an ancient gold dragon, and his breath is a collage of rainbow flames that can sear anything.

Azh'Tharoth has been alive for millenia. His wisdom knows no bounds, and his magic is unparalleled. He is closer to a god or diety than he is to a typical dragon. Azh'Tharoth often sleeps for centuries at a time. However, he, along with many other ancient dragons, were woken in 442 BC, when [[The Rumbling]] was enacted by the [[Infernal Order]]. 

After Azh'Tharoth raised the [[Bastion of Anatha]] to free the dragons, he vanished below the sea, where he remains dormant to this day.
