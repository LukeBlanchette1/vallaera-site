Alcyrus is a powerful mage admitted into [[The League of All-Stars]] alongside the rest of his team, the [[Cobbled Forged]]. However, every other member of his team recently died in the [[Manshu Incident]]. Now, Alcyrus resides alone in their old base in [[Ravenholt]]. Rumors say he's gone a bit mad in their absence. While people know better than to mess with a mad mage, the bounty of riches and magic items in their base have begun to tempt people.
