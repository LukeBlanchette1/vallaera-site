One of the most famous heroes in Thalrune, and considered to be one of the strongest fighters in Anatha. He is a member of the [[The League of All-Stars]], and resides in [[Springshade]]. 

He is an incredible martial fighter, however, his real power comes from his mysterious aura of antimagic around him. He refuses to explain why or how he got this trait, but any and all magic within 10ft of him ceases to function. He is the bane of spellcasters, and his antimagic aura allows him to overcome enemies that he otherwise should not be able to defeat. 

The Storm Herald is very private. He is a brown-haired man with a green cloak and a massive greathammer, but not much else is known about his appearance. His real name is not public knowledge, and very few know his whereabouts at a given moment. However, whenever [[Anatha]] needs him, the Storm Herald is never far away.

The Storm Herald wields the legendary warhammer [[The Ties That Bind]].
