Kas is a dragonborn warrior that has taken [[Anathor's League]] by storm. A newcomer to the competition, he blew all his opponents out of the water with ease. His unmatched swordmanship, fierce fiery breath, and boundless endurance has led him to win Anathor's League two years in a row. He is currently favored by the odds to win a third year, meaning he will face off against the legendary [[Storm Herald]] to determine who is Anathor's Champion.
