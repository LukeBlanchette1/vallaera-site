Lord Drakonhart is the stoic and regal ruler of [[Dragonskeep]]. His chiseled face rarely shows any sign of emotion, and his pragmatic policies match his disposition. However, Lord Drakonhart is kind and does care deeply about his constituents. He values physical prowess and has a powerful militia trained to defend Dragonskeep. He is close with [[Malan Gildhart]], the ruler of [[Huldar's Reach]], since both of their [[Keeps]] are constantly under siege by the [[Warbands]] of the highlands. Dragonskeep is highly fortified and occasionally standoffish due to the frequency of warband attacks.
