A powerful adventuring group themed after purple flowers. They are one of the premier adventuring groups in [[Thalrune]], and have started to gain widespread recognition. They are most famous for their recent victory against the now deceased vampire lord [[Count Augustine]]. Despite their power and fame, the group has recently been tied up in controversies, as many have reported their lack empathy and abuse of their positions of power.

The Four Members of the Troupe are:

Delphinium

Crocus

Aster

Wisteria
