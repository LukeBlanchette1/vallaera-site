A common namesake for the region encompassing the western Keeps of [[Huldar's Reach]], [[Ravenholt]], and [[Dunholm]]. All these [[Keeps]] have the same characteristic thick forests and abundance of magical beings. The Holt produces the majority of the precious metals and rarities for Thalrune, and it is also considered more mysterious and fantastical than the rest of Thalrune. 

The rest of [[Thalrune]] ([[Dragonskeep]], [[Elderspire]], [[Hearthfall]], and [[Varghold]]) are collectively known as [[The Moor]].
