The Krusguard are a loose confederation of warriors and adventurers who seek to maintain the sanctity of the Material Plane. They are denoted by their signature purple berets. They battle against extraplanar invaders and ensure the commonfolk’s safety in Vallaera. The Krusguard can be found across Vallaera but has no centralized authority. Traditionally, a Krusguard chapter is created in a local hub and adventurers take on jobs for their chapter. The Krusguard are looked upon favorably by many in Vallaera and are seen as an honorable alternative to becoming a guard for most people.
