Stoutfoot Grey is the most prominent and powerful werewolf that lives in [[Ravenholt]]. He leads a pack of werewolves that attack travelers and infect them with lycanthropy. He has single-handedly turned lycanthropy from a very rare disease into a full-blown pandemic. His attacks have caused lycanthropy cases to skyrocket in past years, earning him the hefty bounty on his head. Grey and his goons are last known to be hiding out in an abandoned castle in Ravenholt.
