Elderspire is located in [[Thalrune]].

**Elderspire** is the capital of Thalrune, located on a sloped plateau. It is where the king resides, and it is where the Royal Army is kept. It is full of strong, elite warriors. The surrounding lands are full of thin wooded groves and sloping cliffs. There are few monsters or bandits, but the ones that do travel around are quite dangerous.
