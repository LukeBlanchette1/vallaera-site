Kiran, Dragonscourge is a fearsome warrior. He is most famous for leading rebel cells that fight back against the [[Mindrak Dynasty]]. 

Kiran is infamous for his assault on [[Mindrak]]'s castle. Kiran infiltrated the castle, bypassed Mindrak's magical protections, and murdered Mindrak's son, [[Mindrak the Lesser]]. Kiran now wears Mindrak's son as a cape wherever he goes. 

Kiran's siege is considered one of the only public humiliations Mindrak has suffered, and it inspired countless commoners to rise up against Mindrak. Kiran is an idol of freedom and liberation for many of them. Mindrak makes no effort to hide his loathing of Kiran. Murdering his son and being the face of the movement to overthrow him has led Mindrak to put one of the largest bounties ever on Kiran's head.  

Kiran wields the legendary spear [[Flesh Memories]].
